One short line about office life, built for make.fun's "The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head" bounty ($2 in USDC, judged head to head by an AI judge). This is the v2 re-submit: the v1 entry (second monitor, second meeting) scored only 32.02% in round 2, so v2 changes the angle completely. The setup is a reply-all sent to stop the reply-alls; the turn escalates in three beats — 214 unread emails, a warning from HR — and the punchline lands on the last words: somehow, a fan club.

The joke:

> I hit reply-all to ask everyone to stop hitting reply-all, and now I have 214 unread emails, a warning from HR, and somehow a fan club.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the single-line joke (135 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
